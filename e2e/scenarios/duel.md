# Rendered two-browser duel

## Scenario: two players complete a rendered keyboard duel with shared authoritative results

Independent desktop and narrow browser contexts create/join through the real interface,
keep one authenticated socket across a language change, ready, and enter the same round.
Actual compositor screenshots prove the WebGL arena draws varied pixels. Keyboard movement
works only with arena focus, clears on blur, and does not move the actor from a focused form
control. Space auto-repeat plants one bomb; its authoritative explosion removes a soft block,
eliminates its owner, and gives both browsers the same result and matching public snapshots.
Axe checks lobby, waiting, running, and result; screenshots capture desktop/narrow layouts,
including reduced motion and a 320-pixel result layout; no layout overflows horizontally.
Both browsers show authoritative capacity/range/speed and available bomb counts before
placement, during the fuse, and after detonation, alongside alive/eliminated player status.
