# Landmine production acceptance

## Scenario: two browsers observe one persistent mine and its contact detonation

Enable only Landmine and reveal the deterministic pickup at cell (3,1). Collect and place it at
that cell, verify the initially overlapping owner remains safe, then leave fully to cell (2,1).
Capture English desktop and Polish reduced-motion mobile views before the contact window. Both
peers retain the same visible Contact Standard bomb past its placement tick plus the ordinary
180-tick fuse and three more ticks, with no detonation or extra manual action. A normal held-right
movement re-enters the footprint and both peers observe the same ID detonate, actual flames, one
elimination, and the round result without any new placement action.

Both recipients retain the original regular bomb capacity and zero occupied regular slots while
the mine is armed and after contact detonation; the consumed weapon item is not restored.
