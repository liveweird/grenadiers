## Scenario: two browsers share configured rules that drive speed, fuse and match target

The creator first observes a fresh room with every available item enabled and verifies the visible
**All on** / **All off** and **Włącz wszystkie** / **Wyłącz wszystkie** controls. Two browser
contexts then join one room after the creator
selects the Classic preset. The settings editor chooses maximum starting speed, a narrow
turning tolerance, one round win, and a one-second bomb fuse through the visible panel. Both
clients receive the same authoritative settings before becoming ready. The live round then
proves the configured actor speed and fuse deadline, one win ends the match, and the accepted
settings remain in place after both players start a rematch.
