## Scenario: two browsers share configured rules that drive speed, fuse and match target

Two browser contexts join one room. The settings editor chooses maximum starting speed,
a narrow turning tolerance, one round win, and a one-second bomb fuse through the visible
panel. Both clients receive the same authoritative settings before becoming ready. The live
round then proves the configured actor speed and fuse deadline, one win ends the match, and
the accepted settings remain in place after both players start a rematch.
