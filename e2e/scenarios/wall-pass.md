# Wall Pass

## Scenario: Wall Pass crosses one intact block, rejects planting and hard walls, and resumes inside

Two Chromium contexts use pinned seed bytes through generator3 with only Wall Pass enabled.
The English desktop and Polish 320-pixel reduced-motion clients ready before play and reveal the
pickup with an ordinary bomb. After the bomb and flames clear, both painted canvases provide
localized pre-collection captures while both actors are idle; the mobile view has no horizontal
overflow. The holder then collects Wall Pass and moves into the intact block at `(3,2)`. Both
public views retain the opaque block and show the current equipment cue without exposing concealed
contents, while paired pixel and position assertions prove the crossing. A bomb action inside
creates no bomb or event and consumes no inventory. Movement west
clamps at the hard wall `(2,2)`. A strictly newer ordinary reconnect admission retains the
equipment and position, after which normal movement exits north. The scenario uses native keys,
ordinary UI actions, and read-only socket observation. A legal bomb placed after exit has a later entity ID, identical on both clients. The rejected
action creates no bomb or event and leaves resources unchanged; focused core tests verify that it
also consumes no entity allocation. Equipment-loss exit grace remains covered
by core and protocol tests because the sole-item fixture has no replacement pickup.
